Know what you’re
looking at.

Good decisions start with clear information. Explore what to ask about a product, its records and the requirements for your destination.

01

Confirm what’s listed.

Check the compound name, stated form and nominal vial content. Ask which lot-specific records are available and review their method, date and lot identifier together.

02

Understand each blend.

For blends, request the full ingredient list and amount of each component. Confirm whether available records cover the finished blend or only its individual ingredients.

03

Check your destination.

Availability and import requirements vary by location. Include your country and postal code in a quote request so applicable delivery details can be confirmed in writing.
